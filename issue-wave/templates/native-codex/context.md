## Keeping your context lean (this is a regular-mode wave)

Read logs with `tail` or `rg`, and batch independent read-only tool calls. Keep the managed
session IDs and outstanding request IDs across compaction so you can observe their actual
terminal results. Send concise milestones to the coordinator with `send_message`; use
`wait_agent` when waiting for dispatch. Go straight from merge to the after-merge brainstorm
and the final hand-back.
